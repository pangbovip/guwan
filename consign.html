Working with makers

Wen Gu Hall maintains long-term partnerships with jade carvers, zisha artisans, painters and related makers. We source handmade pieces directly and list them on this Site as one-of-a-kind works.

If you are a maker of handmade jade, zisha teaware, porcelain, calligraphy or ink painting and would like to introduce your work, please contact us. We do not publish commission rates, intake quotas or a formal public consign programme on this page — those details are discussed case by case.
